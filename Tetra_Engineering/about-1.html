Meet the team

We always work with a great number of people.

Mr. Sagar Deshmukh

Managing Director & CEO

Mr. Sagar Deshmukh is an experienced MEP engineering professional with over 10 years of expertise in design and project management. He holds a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering, an MBA in Operations, and a Diploma in MEP Design. As an IGBC Accredited Professional, he leads Tetra Engineering Corporation with a focus on innovation, sustainability, quality, and efficient engineering solutions.

Mr. Prashant Jadhav

Associate Consultant

Mr. Prashant Jadhav is a highly experienced MEP project management consultant with over 40 years of industry expertise. At Tetra Engineering Corporation, he guides project execution and strengthens technical capabilities through team training. His extensive Gulf experience and specialization in HVAC consultancy include designing and executing prestigious projects while contributing to fire protection and air-conditioning systems.
